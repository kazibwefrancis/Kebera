# Provider customer care contacts (East Africa)

Last checked: **21 November 2026**. Numbers change, so always confirm on the provider's
official website, app or SIM pack. The same list appears on the Kebera website and
lives in [`site/providers.json`](../site/providers.json). Update that file to correct it
(this page is generated from it).

> ⚠️ Providers will **never** ask for your PIN, password or one-time code. Hang up on
> anyone who does.

**Checked** column: *Official site* = from the provider's own website · *Regulator list* =
from the national regulator's published list · *Third-party* = from a news site or
directory, not yet confirmed by the provider · *Not verified* = please help confirm.

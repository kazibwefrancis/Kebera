# WhatsApp message template

Short enough for WhatsApp. Uses WhatsApp formatting (`*bold*`). Replace the
[brackets] and delete lines that don't apply. Don't include SIM numbers, IMEI,
passwords or your full public IP.

---

Hello [Provider] 👋

I'm having an ongoing problem with my *[5G / 4G / fibre] home internet* (line/account: [number]).

*The problem:* The internet keeps [cutting out / going very slow / lagging] on *all* my devices. It comes and goes, mostly [in the evenings / at random].

*What I've checked (with a diagnostic tool):*
✅ My devices and Wi-Fi are working fine
✅ My router and [outdoor unit / modem] respond without losing data
✅ [The outdoor unit's signal strength is good: RSRP [value] dBm]

*What I found:* The delays and drops happen *after* my equipment, on the network side.
[Internet loss [x]%, delay spikes up to [y] ms, jitter [z] ms.]
[Signal quality is poor (SINR [value] dB) although strength is good, which points to congestion or interference at the tower.]
[The connection dropped and reconnected at [time].]

*Please can you:*
1. Check the [tower / line] serving my area
2. Tell me if my line is being slowed under fair usage [I've used about [x] GB this month]
3. [Re-aim / check my outdoor unit] [Give my line a public IP address]

Please log a complaint and send me a *reference number*. Thank you 🙏

[Name]
[Area]

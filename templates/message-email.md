# Email template

Replace the [brackets]. Attach `report.md` or `report.html` from your Kebera
report folder if you like. They contain no SIM numbers, MAC addresses,
neighbour Wi-Fi names or full public IP.

---

**Subject:** Ongoing [slow / dropping] internet: line [number], [area]

Hello [Provider] team,

I'm writing about an ongoing problem with my [5G / 4G / fibre] home internet
(account/line: [number]).

**The problem**
For [how long], my internet has been unreliable on every device in my home.
Sometimes it cuts out completely; other times it is connected but very slow or
laggy. It is worst [in the evenings / at random times].

**What I have checked**
I used Kebera, an open-source diagnostic tool, which tests each part of the
connection at the same moment:

- My devices and Wi-Fi: healthy ([x]% loss to my router)
- My [outdoor unit / modem] and the cable to it: healthy ([x]% loss)
- The internet beyond my equipment: [x]% loss, delay up to [y] ms, jitter [z] ms
- [Signal at my unit: RSRP [a] dBm (good strength), SINR [b] dB (poor quality)]
- [Speed: [d] Mbps download, [u] Mbps upload]

**Conclusion:** the problem is beyond my equipment, on the network side.

**Requests**
1. Please check the [tower / line] serving my area for congestion or faults.
2. Please confirm whether my line is limited for any reason (for example a
   fair-usage threshold). My equipment shows about [x] GB used this month.
3. [Please check the alignment of my outdoor unit / send a technician.]
4. [Please provide a public IP address for my line, and tell me any cost.]

Please log this as a complaint and send me a reference number.

Thank you,
[Name]
[Phone]
[Area / location]

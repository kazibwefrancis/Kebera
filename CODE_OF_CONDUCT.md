# Code of conduct

Kebera is for everyone, whatever their technical level, language or provider.

## Expected

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new to this.
- Assume good intent. Explain, don't mock.
- Keep discussions about measurements and fixes, not about attacking people or
  companies.
- Protect privacy: never post anyone's identifiers, passwords or location details.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Sharing other people's private information.
- Using the project to plan unauthorised access to networks or devices, or to
  interfere with radio signals.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content and block contributors who break these
rules. Report problems privately to the maintainer through the contact listed on
the GitHub profile of the repository owner.

# Code of Conduct

Kebera exists to help ordinary people understand their internet connection and get
it fixed. That only works if the community around it is **respectful, honest, safe
and ethical**. This Code of Conduct applies to everyone who takes part: users,
contributors, maintainers, and anyone using Kebera or its AI guides.

By using Kebera, or by contributing to or taking part in this project, you agree to
follow this Code of Conduct and the [Acceptable Use Policy](ACCEPTABLE_USE.md).

---

## 1. Our pledge

We pledge to make Kebera a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, tribe, clan, nationality,
language, religion, gender, gender identity or expression, sexual orientation,
education, technical skill, income, social status, appearance, or which internet
provider they use.

We pledge to keep Kebera what it was built to be: **a tool for diagnosing your own
connection, never a tool for hacking, harm or deception.**

---

## 2. Respect

### Respect people

- **Be kind and patient.** Many people using Kebera aren't technical. There are no
  "stupid questions". Explain; don't mock.
- **Assume good intent,** and ask before assuming the worst.
- **Welcome newcomers,** and help them find their way around.
- **Disagree politely.** Criticise ideas and code, never the person.
- **Accept feedback gracefully,** and give it constructively.
- **Respect every language.** Contributions and questions in Luganda, Swahili,
  Runyankole, Acholi, Lusoga, English or any other language are equally welcome.
- **Respect people's time.** Keep issues focused, search before posting, and follow
  up on what you start.

### Respect provider staff

Kebera helps you complain **effectively**, not aggressively.

- **Be courteous to customer care agents and technicians.** The person on the phone
  usually didn't cause the problem. Firm and factual works; insults and threats don't.
- **Never harass, threaten, dox or publicly shame** individual employees of any
  provider or manufacturer.
- **Criticise services with evidence,** not with personal attacks or rumours.

### Respect the project

- **Follow the maintainer's decisions** on what is merged. Only the maintainer
  merges changes into the official repository.
- **Respect the licence and attribution.** Kebera is MIT-licensed. Keep copyright
  and licence notices when you copy or adapt it.
- **Don't spam,** advertise, or post off-topic promotions in issues, pull requests
  or discussions.

---

## 3. Ethical use: Kebera is not a hacking tool

These rules apply to **how you use Kebera**, **what you contribute**, and **what you
ask AI agents to do with it**.

### Always

- Use Kebera **only on your own connection**, or one you have the owner's clear
  permission to check.
- **Sign in to your own equipment yourself,** with your own credentials.
- **Tell the truth** in reports and complaints. Report what was actually measured.
- **Protect people's privacy:** yours, your neighbours', and everyone else's.
- **Follow the law** in your country, and in the country where the network is.

### Never

- **Hack, or attempt to hack,** any network, router, account or device.
- Access, scan, probe or test **anyone else's network or equipment** without permission.
- **Guess, crack, share or brute-force passwords,** use default-password lists, or
  bypass any login.
- **Exploit vulnerabilities,** use hidden admin functions, or tamper with firmware.
- **Capture or intercept** other people's traffic, or join other people's Wi-Fi
  without permission.
- **Jam or interfere with radio signals,** or promote jammers or illegal boosters.
- **Collect, publish or sell** other people's data: network names, device
  identifiers, locations, SIM numbers or account details.
- **Fabricate, alter or exaggerate** test results to deceive a provider, a court, an
  insurer or anyone else, or use Kebera reports for fraud or false refund claims.
- **Impersonate** a provider, manufacturer, regulator or the Kebera project, for
  example with fake "official" versions, fake support pages or phishing.
- **Modify Kebera to remove its safeguards** and present it as Kebera, or market a
  modified copy as a hacking tool.
- **Instruct AI agents** to do any of the above, or try to trick Kebera's agent
  instructions into doing it.

**Misuse or perversion of Kebera is not tolerated.** Reports of misuse are taken
seriously, and illegal activity may be reported to the relevant authorities.

---

## 4. Privacy and safety in the community

- **Remove identifiers before posting** logs, screenshots or reports: SIM/phone
  numbers, IMEI/IMSI/ICCID, serial numbers, MAC addresses, account numbers,
  passwords, Wi-Fi passwords, full public IP addresses and home locations.
- If you see someone's private details posted, **don't repeat them.** Tell the
  maintainer so they can be removed.
- **Don't ask people for passwords,** remote access to their devices, or money.
  Kebera support never requires any of these.
- **Physical safety comes first.** Never encourage anyone to climb roofs or masts,
  open sealed equipment, or handle power or fibre. That's work for technicians.

---

## 5. Honest and responsible contributions

- **Contribute only what you have the right to share.** Don't paste code, text or
  images you don't have permission to use.
- **Be transparent about AI-generated contributions.** Review and test them, and
  say what you tested and on which equipment.
- **Don't submit changes that weaken safety,** privacy protections or the read-only
  design. Such pull requests are closed.
- **Report security problems privately,** through GitHub's private vulnerability
  reporting (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)), not in public issues.
- **Keep provider contact information accurate.** Link to an official source when
  you add or correct a number.

---

## 6. Unacceptable behaviour

Examples include:

- Harassment, intimidation, threats, stalking or violent language
- Insults, slurs, or discriminatory jokes or remarks, including tribal, ethnic,
  religious, gender-based or disability-based ones
- Sexual language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, deliberately derailing discussions, or repeated off-topic posting
- Publishing anyone's private information (doxxing)
- Encouraging or helping anyone break this Code or the Acceptable Use Policy
- Any of the "Never" items in section 3

---

## 7. Enforcement

The maintainer is responsible for enforcing this Code of Conduct, and may remove,
edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that break it.

| Step | When | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Correction** | Minor or first-time issue, such as unkind wording or posting an identifier by mistake | A private note explaining the problem; the content may be edited or removed |
| **2. Warning** | A repeated issue, or one more serious incident | A written warning with consequences; no interaction with those involved for a set time |
| **3. Temporary ban** | A serious breach, or continued behaviour after a warning | Banned from the project's spaces for a set period |
| **4. Permanent ban** | Harassment, hacking or misuse, fraud, doxxing, or a pattern of serious breaches | Permanently banned; misuse or illegal activity may be reported to the platform and relevant authorities |

Serious breaches, such as misuse for hacking, threats or doxxing, can go straight
to a permanent ban.

---

## 8. Reporting

If you experience or see behaviour that breaks this Code:

- **Security problems or misuse of Kebera:** use **Report a vulnerability** on the
  repository's **Security** tab. It's private.
- **Conduct problems:** contact the maintainer privately through the contact
  details on the repository owner's GitHub profile.

Include what happened, where (links), and when. Reports are handled privately and as
quickly as possible. Retaliation against anyone who reports in good faith is itself
a breach of this Code.

---

## 9. Scope

This Code applies in all Kebera spaces (the repository, issues, pull requests,
discussions, the website and any chat groups), when someone represents the project
in public, and to how Kebera and its AI guides are used.

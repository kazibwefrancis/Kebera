How it works

Every link checked at the same moment

Your internet is a chain. Kebera pings every link of it twice a second, all at once. When something fails, the first link that fails is where the problem is.

Router fails first

The problem is inside your home: Wi-Fi or router. Usually free and quick to fix yourself.

Outdoor unit fails

That unit, its cable or its power. Check connections and power; the provider replaces faulty units.

Only the internet fails

The provider's network: tower, fibre line or congestion. Send them your report.

What it checks

Everything a good technician would look at

📶 Wi-Fi

Signal, 2.4 vs 5 GHz, neighbours crowding your channel, and the best channel to use.

🔎 Your equipment

Recognises ZTE, Huawei, TP-Link, MikroTik, Nokia, FiberHome and more from the pages they already show.

📉 Stability

Loss, delay, lag spikes and jitter on every link at once, and where each failure happened.

📡 Mobile signal

Strength (RSRP) and quality (SINR, RSRQ). Tells a busy tower apart from a badly placed unit.

🧲 Interference and jamming

Wi-Fi crowding, plus the 4G check that separates real interference from congestion.

⏱️ Long log

Records every 5 seconds for hours to catch drops that come and go, and shows the worst hours.

🚀 Speed and DNS

Real download and upload, and whether a faster DNS would open sites quicker.

🎮 Strict NAT

Detects when your provider shares one public address between customers (CGNAT).

📝 Reports

An HTML report, a detailed 8-section report, and a WhatsApp-ready message, with your private details left out.

A verdict you can act on

Not just numbers: Kebera says where the problem is, what you can fix yourself, and what the provider must fix, with the evidence attached.

Is it safe?

A diagnostic tool, not a hacking tool

🛡️ Your connection only

It finds your own router and outdoor unit automatically and has no option to aim it anywhere else. A built-in safety check stops it if it tries.

👀 Read-only

It never changes settings, never logs in, never guesses passwords. You sign in to your own equipment yourself.

🔒 Private

Everything stays on your computer. Reports leave out neighbours' Wi-Fi names, MAC and IMEI numbers, and your full public IP.

📖 Read every line

Open source under the MIT licence. Plain, readable scripts, safeguards in plain view, and changes reviewed by the maintainer.

Misuse or perversion of Kebera is not tolerated. Using it on networks you don't own or aren't permitted to test may be a crime under laws such as Uganda's Computer Misuse Act and equivalents worldwide. Read the acceptable use policy.

FAQ

Questions people ask

Do I need a paid AI or technical skills?

No. Paste the free chat guide into any free AI chat, double-click the Windows check-up, or follow the step-by-step guide. A paid coding agent just does more of the steps for you.

Can I use only my phone?

Yes, partly. The chat guide has a phone-only path covering router pages, signal readings, a speed test and comparing Wi-Fi with mobile data.

Will it change anything on my router?

No. Kebera is read-only. If you later decide to change something, such as your Wi-Fi channel, you do it yourself, knowing how to undo it.

Is it legal?

Yes, on your own connection. Kebera only does what your computer and browser already do. Using it on other people's networks is not allowed.

Does it work with Airtel, MTN, Safaricom, fibre, Starlink…?

Yes. It tests the connection itself, so it works with any provider, and it recognises ZTE and Huawei equipment (common with mobile providers) most completely.

How much data does it use?

About 35 MB for the standard check, 12 MB for the quick check, under 1 MB without the speed test.

Can Kebera prove my provider is at fault?

It gives strong evidence of where the problem was during the test. It isn't a certified measurement, but it turns "my internet is bad" into specific facts that are hard to dismiss.

What does "Kebera" mean?

"Examine" or "inspect" in Luganda.

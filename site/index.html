LINK 02 · Who it's for

Beyond "restart
your router."

Whether you love the technical detail or just want your internet fixed, Kebera meets you where you are.

If you're tech-savvy

Break it down and diagnose it yourself.

See every link measured at the same moment, read your equipment's signal and logs, and find the exact point of failure.

  • Loss, jitter and lag spikes on every link
  • RSRP, SINR, RSRQ and the interference check
  • Long logs, hour-by-hour patterns and event logs
  • Fix what's yours: Wi-Fi channel, 5 GHz, DNS, cabling
If you're not

Get a report your provider can't ignore.

When all you hear is "restart your router", "switch it off and on" and "we'll call you back", Kebera writes it up for you.

  • A plain-language verdict: home, equipment or provider
  • A detailed report with the evidence attached
  • A WhatsApp-ready message with your numbers filled in
  • Step-by-step escalation if they still don't act
🧰

It doesn't replace a technician.

Some faults need hands on equipment: a damaged cable on the roof, a failing outdoor unit, a fibre fault or a problem at the tower. Kebera tells you which part is failing, so you know when to ask for a technician and exactly what to tell them.

LINK 04 · How it works

Three steps.
One honest answer.

1

Check every link at once

Twice a second, Kebera pings your router, your outdoor unit or modem, and two internet servers, all at the same moment.

2

Read your equipment

It recognises your router and outdoor unit, and reads their signal quality, uptime and logs after you sign in yourself.

3

Get a verdict and a report

Home, equipment or provider: you'll know which, what to fix yourself, and what to send them.

Router fails first

The problem is inside your home. Often free and quick to fix.

Outdoor unit fails

That unit, its cable or its power. Check them, or ask for a replacement.

Only the internet fails

Your provider's network. Send them your report.

LINK 06 · What it checks

Everything a good
technician looks at.

Wi-Fi

Signal, 2.4 vs 5 GHz, neighbours crowding your channel, and the best channel to use.

Your equipment

Recognises ZTE, Huawei, TP-Link, MikroTik, Nokia, FiberHome and more.

Stability

Loss, delay, lag spikes and jitter on every link at once, and where each failure happened.

Mobile signal

Strength (RSRP) and quality (SINR, RSRQ), to tell a busy tower apart from a badly placed unit.

Interference and jamming

Wi-Fi crowding, plus the check that separates real interference from congestion.

Long log

Records every 5 seconds for hours to catch drops that come and go.

Speed and DNS

Real download and upload, and whether a faster DNS would open sites quicker.

Strict NAT

Detects when your provider shares one public address between customers.

Reports

HTML, a detailed report and a WhatsApp message, with private details left out.

LINK 07 · Is it safe?

A diagnostic tool.
Not a hacking tool.

🛡️

Your connection only

It finds your own router and outdoor unit automatically and has no way to aim anywhere else. A built-in safety check stops it if it tries.

👀

Read-only

Never changes settings, never logs in, never guesses passwords. You sign in to your own equipment yourself.

🔒

Private

Everything stays on your computer. Reports leave out neighbours' Wi-Fi names, MAC and IMEI numbers, and your full IP.

📖

Read every line

Open source under the MIT licence: plain scripts, safeguards in plain view, and changes reviewed by the maintainer.

⚠️

Misuse or perversion of Kebera is not tolerated. Using it on networks you don't own or aren't permitted to test may be a crime under Uganda's Computer Misuse Act and similar laws worldwide. Read the acceptable use policy.

Do I need a paid AI or technical skills?

No. Paste the free chat guide into any free AI chat, double-click the Windows check-up, or follow the step-by-step guide. A paid coding agent just does more of the steps for you.

Does it replace a technician?

No. Some faults need hands on equipment: a damaged roof cable, a failing outdoor unit, a fibre fault or a tower problem. Kebera helps you find which part is failing, fix what's yours, and, when a technician is needed, ask for one with clear evidence of what's wrong.

Does it work with fibre, pocket routers and indoor-only units?

Yes. Kebera works with 5G/4G outdoor plus indoor units, indoor-only 4G/5G routers, pocket routers and MiFis, fibre (it checks the fibre modem's light level), any Wi-Fi router, rooftop antennas and Starlink.

Can I use only my phone?

Yes, partly. The chat guide has a phone-only path covering router pages, signal readings, a speed test and comparing Wi-Fi with mobile data.

Will it change anything on my router?

No. Kebera is read-only. If you later decide to change something, such as your Wi-Fi channel, you do it yourself, knowing how to undo it.

Is it legal?

Yes, on your own connection. Kebera only does what your computer and browser already do. Using it on other people's networks is not allowed.

Does it work with Airtel, MTN, Safaricom, fibre, Starlink…?

Yes. It tests the connection itself, so it works with any provider, and it recognises ZTE and Huawei equipment (common with mobile providers) most completely.

How much data does it use?

About 35 MB for the standard check, 12 MB for the quick check, under 1 MB without the speed test.

Can Kebera prove my provider is at fault?

It gives strong evidence of where the problem was during the test. It isn't a certified measurement, but it turns "my internet is bad" into specific facts that are hard to dismiss.
